Project details

Know what to expect.

A few details that help keep the scope, costs, and next steps clear from the beginning.

What counts as a page?

A page is a distinct page on your website with its own URL, such as Home, About, or Services. Extra pages or substantial unique layouts can change the quote. We’ll list the planned pages in the written scope.

What is a revision round?

A revision round is one organized set of feedback on a submitted design or website draft. The first two packages include two rounds. New pages, new features, or a major change in direction are outside the original scope and will be quoted for approval before work continues.

Are writing, branding, hosting, and the domain included?

The listed ranges assume you provide final text and images. Copywriting, logo or brand creation, paid fonts or photography, hosting plans, and domain registration are not included unless the written quote says they are. Third-party charges are paid to their providers.

How are timing and payment decided?

The written proposal will confirm the project scope, estimated schedule, payment schedule, and any agreed launch support before work begins. Timing depends on the project and when content and feedback are provided.

Can we add features or request updates later?

Yes. We specialize in front-end websites built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. We can assess new pages, booking or payment features, forms, integrations, or content updates as separate work. Some features require paid third-party services or back-end development, so we’ll explain the scope and cost and get your approval before starting.

Do you guarantee search rankings or sales?

No website can guarantee a specific ranking, number of leads, or sales. We focus on clear structure, responsive presentation, and an easy path for visitors to learn about your business and contact you.

After launch

Keep your website current.

A website needs occasional attention so its information stays accurate and its key paths keep working. Ongoing care is optional and only billed when it is included in an agreed plan.

Hosting & domain

Hosting makes the site available online; a domain is the address visitors type. Hosting costs depend on the provider, and custom domains usually renew separately. GitHub Pages can host static sites from public repositories on its Free plan; domain registration is still separate.

Updates & check-ins

Optional care can cover small text or image updates, keeping services and project details current, and periodically checking links, contact paths, and mobile layouts. New pages, redesigns, or added features are quoted separately.

Protect the project

Keep domain and hosting accounts in the business owner’s name, renew them on time, and retain a copy of the site files. Paid fonts, images, form services, or other third-party tools may have their own fees and renewal terms.

Other factors that can change a quote include extra pages, copywriting, logo or brand work, original imagery, additional revisions, booking or payment features, migrations, outside integrations, and rush timelines. Any recurring care or provider fees are explained separately.
